# Anti-Goals

## Not building now

- Public profiles, followers, feeds, or discovery.
- Direct messaging outside an event or group context.
- AI planning, AI recap generation, or ranking.
- Billing, teams, admin dashboards, or analytics.
- Full external calendar sync.
- Task/project management modules.
- Broad community or marketplace behavior.

## Product guardrails

- If it does not help people plan, attend, discuss, or remember an event, it does not belong in the MVP.
- Keep create/edit flows fast by default.
- Keep memories derived from completed events, not free-floating posts.
- Favor realistic fixture-backed behavior over fake placeholder features.
